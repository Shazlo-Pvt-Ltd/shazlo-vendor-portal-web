# Security policy

## Supported versions

This repository has no versioned releases. Security fixes go to `main` and to what is deployed from it.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub:
<https://github.com/Shazlo-Pvt-Ltd/shazlo-vendor-portal-web/security/advisories/new>

Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a vulnerability.

A report should include:

- the affected part: the service, app or package, and the endpoint or screen;
- steps to reproduce, with any request, payload or account role they need;
- the impact: what an attacker could read, change or stop.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge a report within 3 business days.
- We send an update at least once a week until it is fixed.

## Scope

In scope: `services/`, `apps/`, `packages/`, and the legacy `app/` and `backend/`.

Out of scope: `services/central-stub/`, which is never deployed.
